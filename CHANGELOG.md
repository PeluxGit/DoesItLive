## [Unreleased]

First release.

### Added

- A block on health bars showing the healing your Rejuvenation, Regrowth, Wild Growth, Renew and Riptide still have to do, after current health and incoming heals.
- Works on EllesmereUI, Blizzard, BetterBlizzFrames and ElvUI/oUF frames, and on friendly nameplates (EllesmereUI, Platynator, Threat Plates, Blizzard and others).
- Crits counted at their average, with an option to show only the healing you're sure of.
- Options window (`/dil`) with a live preview and four presets: Minimal, Classic, Juicy and Debug.
